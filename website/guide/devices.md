# SteelSeries devices

Inari drives SteelSeries hardware directly over USB — no root, no SteelSeries
GG. Each tab is presence-aware: it shows a friendly "not connected" state
instead of failing when the device is absent.

::: info
Device support is Linux-only and specific to the author's hardware. The HID
protocols were learned from
[HeadsetControl](https://github.com/Sapd/HeadsetControl),
[elegos/Linux-Arctis-Manager](https://github.com/elegos/Linux-Arctis-Manager)
and [loteran/Arctis-Sound-Manager](https://github.com/loteran/Arctis-Sound-Manager).
Not affiliated with or endorsed by SteelSeries.
:::

## Headset — Arctis Nova Pro Wireless

Full control of the base station: live battery and charge-slot level, ANC /
transparency, sidetone, mic volume and mute-LED brightness, a 10-band hardware
EQ (with presets), auto-off, wireless speed/range, line-out mode, an anti-crackle
headroom toggle, and a ChatMix read-out.

![Headset](/headset.png)

## OLED — the base station's 128×64 display

Drive the display with:

- **Live modes** — VU meters, ChatMix, FFT spectrum, headset status, CPU/GPU
  graphs, network, uptime/load, temperatures, now playing, album art, clocks,
  timer, weather, mouse battery and active apps, plus a timed rotation.
- **System monitor** — CPU / RAM / GPU.
- **Notifications** — mirror desktop notifications to the panel.
- **Your own media** — images, GIFs and video, scaled and dithered to 1-bit on
  the fly, plus built-in clips and animations.

![OLED](/oled.png)

## Mouse — Aerox 9 Wireless

DPI presets, polling rate, per-zone RGB, reactive click lighting, sleep and dim
timeouts, and battery level.
